Phone Bill Splitter

Drop in your Verizon bill PDF to see what each person owes. Lines that share a phone number (like a watch on Number Share) are combined, and account-wide charges are split evenly between numbers.

Copied

Account-wide charges are divided by the number of distinct phone numbers. When they don't divide evenly to the cent, the first numbers listed pay one extra cent so the shares add up to the bill exactly. Any balance carried over from a previous bill is not included.
